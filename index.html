Who we are

Faith is more than words. It is action.

ChristCares Foundation is a Christian, faith-based, non-profit organization committed to demonstrating the love and compassion of Jesus Christ through practical action.

We reach out to communities and vulnerable individuals, standing with people through difficult circumstances and reminding them that they are seen, valued, and not alone.

What we do

Three pillars. One purpose.

Practical initiatives designed to build healthier, stronger, and more compassionate communities.

Community gathering · Kenya

Pillar 01

Mental Health Awareness

Building awareness, encouraging open conversations, and connecting people with appropriate emotional and psychological support.

  • Community and school awareness talks
  • Stigma-reduction campaigns
  • Wellbeing and stress-management workshops
  • Peer-support circles and referrals
Lamu County · Kenya

Pillar 02

Environmental Conservation

Working with local communities to care for the environment and protect natural resources for future generations.

  • Native tree planting and seedling care
  • Neighbourhood and river clean-ups
  • Waste sorting, recycling, and upcycling
  • Environmental education and stewardship
Kenya · Meal preparation

Pillar 03

Street Feeding & Community Outreach

Sharing food and essential supplies with people experiencing hunger, while meeting neighbours with dignity and care.

  • Prepare and share fresh meals
  • Food parcels and community food drives
  • Water, hygiene kits, and essentials
  • Volunteer-led street and family outreach
